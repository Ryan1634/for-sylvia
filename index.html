Dear Sylvia

Sylvia,

There's something I want you to understand, and I hope you'll take a moment to read it.

When I told you that I've had feelings for you for about a year, I meant every word. Even when you were in another relationship, my feelings never really went away. I won't pretend I didn't hope that someday things would be different between us. When your previous relationship ended, a part of me hoped that maybe we'd finally get an opportunity to see where things could go.

I wasn't waiting because I had nothing else to do or because I couldn't find someone else. It's simply that my feelings for you were genuine, and I never really found myself wanting to pursue anyone else. I understand why that might be difficult for you to believe, but that's the truth.

I've enjoyed getting closer to you, our conversations, the little jokes, the affection and the moments we've shared. I genuinely wanted to build something meaningful with you. You became someone I could picture in my future, someone I hoped to share more than just beautiful moments with.

And perhaps that's what makes this difficult. I'm not just letting go of what we had; I'm also letting go of the future I quietly imagined we could have had. A part of me will probably always wonder what things might have been like if circumstances and feelings had been different.

I never wanted things to end this way. I wish love alone could have been enough to bring two people together and keep them there. But I've come to understand that feelings, no matter how genuine, cannot make two people ready for the same thing at the same time.

I also understand that healing isn't something anyone can rush. I don't want you to feel pressured to love me, choose me or make promises you're not ready to keep. Your feelings matter too, and I respect that.

At the same time, I have to be honest with myself. I can't comfortably pretend that we're just friends when my feelings are much deeper than that. As much as it hurts, I would rather take a step back and give myself room to move forward than remain close while secretly hoping things will change.

Please don't feel guilty about any of this. I don't regret loving you, and I don't regret giving us a chance. If anything, I'm grateful for the moments we shared and for the opportunity to get to know you the way I did.

I never wanted a goodbye with you, Sylvia. But sometimes, caring deeply about someone also means accepting that you cannot force a place in their heart. And sometimes, the hardest thing is walking away from someone you still wish you could stay with.

If I ever cross your mind, I hope you'll remember that someone genuinely cared about you, believed in the possibility of something beautiful with you, and once wished for nothing more than the chance to build a lifetime together.

I sincerely wish you healing, peace and happiness. Take good care of yourself, Sylvia.

With care, Ryan. ♡
